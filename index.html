Use case 1 of 6 · Market analysis

Turn a pile of market reports into one cited brief

Why it exists

Analysts read dozens of reports and notes to answer one question, and confidential text could not go into consumer AI tools.

Who uses it

Market analysts, traders and strategy.

Where it came from

A national education nonprofit built it, with advice from Glean. P66 version reads SharePoint, OneDrive and Outlook.

Customer-authored · measured~1 day → ~20 min

To synthesize 300+ open-text survey comments. That is survey text, not market reports, so P66’s gain will differ.

Source: Glean customer use case registry, Aug 2026. Customer names withheld.Glean5

Use case 2 of 6 · Desk productivity

Start each day with a ranked brief, not a full inbox

Why it exists

Priorities sit across calendar, inbox and chat, so people assemble their own morning briefing by hand.

Who uses it

Trading desk, treasury, middle office, anyone with a heavy inbox.

Where it came from

A recruiting software company built it, with advice from Glean. P66 version reads Outlook Calendar, Outlook mail and Teams.

Self-reported · pilot survey25 min per day

Saved per person, from one pilot survey; modeled at about $1.56M a year. Not instrumented, so treat it as directional.

Source: Glean customer use case registry, Aug 2026. Customer names withheld.Glean6

Use case 3 of 6 · Middle office and compliance

Check every contract against your own standards

Why it exists

Staff dig through old agreements by hand to find the key terms and decide what to do next.

Who uses it

Middle office, compliance, legal and commercial.

Where it came from

A services firm that answers RFPs built it. P66 version compares against a SharePoint playbook and past agreements.

Customer-verified · published92% faster

RFP responses; 30+ hours saved per 100 responses and data extraction 92.5% faster. A companion agent cut capability analysis effort by 83%.

Source: Glean customer use case registry, Aug 2026. Customer names withheld.Glean7

Use case 4 of 6 · HR, IT and finance operations

Answer routine questions before they become tickets

Why it exists

The same policy questions arrive all day, and a small team answers each one by hand.

Who uses it

HR, IT service desk and finance operations.

Where it came from

A global online marketplace’s BizTech team built it with Glean. It ran in Slack; the P66 version would use SharePoint and ServiceNow knowledge.

Realized in production68% fewer tickets

HR ticket volume fell 68%, with 93% answer accuracy against an 80% benchmark and 2 to 3 hours a week saved per support employee.

Source: Glean customer use case registry, Aug 2026. Customer names withheld.Glean8

Use case 5 of 6 · Operations and compliance

Refresh stale procedures in a standard template

Why it exists

Procedures drift out of date, and keeping hundreds current by hand would need a dedicated hire.

Who uses it

Middle and back office, compliance and IT operations.

Where it came from

A digital banking software provider built it. A biotech built a similar SOP generator as a skill plus an agent.

Customer-verified90% rewritten

Share of existing runbook content rewritten by agents. The team plans to refresh 75% of its documents every 90 days without a dedicated hire.

Use case 6 of 6 · Credit and counterparty risk

Draft the credit memo; the analyst makes the call
